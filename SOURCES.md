# Sources — every fact on the front page, 20 November 2026

All stories happened on, or were first reported on, Friday 20 November 2026.
Figures are as reported that day.

1. **Russian strikes hit three Kyiv bridges** — Just Security, Early
   Edition 20 Nov (BBC: Mayor Vitali Klitschko said overnight strikes
   targeted bridges in Kyiv and killed eight people; Prime Minister
   Serhiy Koretsky said 28 injured across attacks on nine regions; a
   strike on a railway station in Lviv killed two).
2. **Russia violates Polish airspace** — Just Security, Early Edition
   20 Nov (Reuters: Defence Minister Władysław Kosiniak-Kamysz said
   Russia violated Poland's airspace overnight with three objects,
   allegedly two missiles and one drone, as Poland prepared Coalition
   of the Willing exercises).
3. **Houthi missile hits Riyadh tower; tanker fire in Hormuz** — One
   Turn, 20 Nov (Houthi missiles damaged the Public Investment Fund
   tower in Riyadh's financial district; Iran war in its ninth month,
   a tanker caught fire after hitting a mine in the Strait of Hormuz;
   Brent crude $90.05).
4. **Estonia closes border with Russia** — Just Security, Early
   Edition 20 Nov (Reuters: Estonia said it would close its border
   with Russia for at least six months; Russian Foreign Ministry
   spokeswoman Maria Zakharova accused Estonia of planning a new
   "Iron Curtain").
5. **Iraq moves on Kataib Hezbollah** — Just Security, Early Edition
   20 Nov (Reuters: Iraqi security forces deployed in Jurf al-Sakhar,
   a stronghold of Iran-aligned Kataib Hezbollah, under a deal to limit
   the group, accused of running an oil-smuggling network).
6. **Israeli forces cross Yellow Line in Gaza** — One Turn, 20 Nov
   (Israeli troops crossed the ceasefire boundary in central Gaza,
   the first such incursion since the truce).
7. **Stocks fall for a fifth day** — One Turn, 20 Nov (S&P 500 lost
   about 2%, its fifth straight session of losses; Japan's Nikkei
   down 4.8%; gold fell 4.2%).
8. **COP31 draft drops fossil-fuel phase-out** — One Turn, 20 Nov
   (negotiators removed any reference to ending fossil fuel use from
   a slimmed-down draft deal at COP31 in Antalya, Turkey).
9. **Kenya discharges last Ebola patient** — One Turn, 20 Nov
   (Kenya discharged its last Ebola patient, 37 days after its first
   case; Kenya begins a 42-day countdown to an Ebola-free declaration).
10. **Cyclone Akvan makes landfall in Odisha** — One Turn / 20 Nov
    briefings (Cyclone Akvan made landfall on India's eastern coast,
    in Odisha).
11. **BepiColombo reaches Mercury** — One Turn, 20 Nov (BepiColombo
    spacecraft reaches Mercury after eight years).
12. **Anthropic closes 17% above IPO price** — One Turn, 20 Nov
    (Anthropic closed 17% above its IPO price in its Nasdaq debut).
